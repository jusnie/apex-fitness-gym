# Apex Fitness Gym Product Plan

## Product direction

Apex Fitness Gym is a premium black, red, and white fitness companion. It prioritizes a rapid workout flow while evolving into a full platform for optional cloud sync, profiles, sharing, challenges, leaderboards, and coach/client collaboration.

## Experience priorities

1. **Fast training flow** — open the app, choose a routine or muscles, log a set, and continue with minimal friction.
2. **Reliable local-first data** — workouts and core progress remain useful without a connection.
3. **Premium Apex identity** — Default and Liquid Glass Beta themes apply consistently across the product.
4. **Consent-led connected features** — accounts, sync, social sharing, and coaching remain opt-in and transparent.
5. **Safe Android integrations** — home-screen widgets must stay memory-conscious and never compromise app stability.

## Near-term work

- Strengthen account and Supabase synchronization flows.
- Complete shareable profiles, challenges, and leaderboard experiences.
- Extend coach/client permissions, planning, and feedback workflows.
- Add release automation once secure signing credentials are available.
- Maintain APK download, complete source availability, and legal notices with every public release.

## Release policy

Public source and distributed builds remain GPLv3. Required upstream attribution and CC BY-SA exercise-artwork credits are maintained in the repository's legal documents and in the app's Legal Notices area.
